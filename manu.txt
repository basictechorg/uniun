Investigated the floating nav, Shiv input composer, Brahma graph FAB, and home
shell.

Root cause (same in both spots): FloatingNav pads its bottom by
MediaQuery.padding.bottom (home-indicator safe area), so its total height grows
on home-indicator devices (~88px). But both overlay elements used hardcoded
bottom offsets that ignored that inset:
  - Shiv input: fixed 80.0  → overlaps the taller nav.
  - Brahma FAB: fixed bottom: 96 → nearly touches it.

Fixes (surgical, safe-area aware):
  - lib/features/shiv/chat/widgets/shiv_input_composer.dart
      closed-keyboard pad 80.0 → 80.0 + MediaQuery.padding.bottom
  - lib/features/brahma/graph/pages/graph_page.dart
      FAB bottom: 96 → 96 + MediaQuery.of(context).padding.bottom

No change on devices without a home indicator (padding.bottom == 0).
